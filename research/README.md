# Research & Content

This folder contains the raw materials and curated content that power the **ExamSense** prototype. This includes syllabus information, practice questions, and educational resources.

## Contents

* **Syllabi**: Official WASSCE syllabus documents for our target subjects (e.g., Mathematics, English).
* **Practice Questions**: A collection of multiple-choice questions mapped to specific topics within the syllabus. For the prototype, this will be a static list.
* **Resource Curation**: A list of links to free, high-quality educational resources (YouTube videos, articles, guides) that will be recommended to students based on their performance.

This content is managed by the Product/Research leads.
